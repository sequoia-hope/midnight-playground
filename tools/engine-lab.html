Engine Sound Lab

A prototype of the physical exhaust model from docs/vision/sound.md: every cylinder fires its own pulse (never two alike) into its own header pipe, the headers merge and ring through the exhaust, muffler and tailpipe. B is this new model; A is the engine in the game today, driven the same way, for comparison. Headphones first, then try the Listen on setting to hear what a phone or laptop would make of it.

Measure (offline, no ears)

Renders every preset at a few rpm / throttle points, new model vs the game's comparable car, and compares the energy in the rumble band (40–150 Hz) with the whine band (1–4 kHz), and cycle corr.: how alike one 720° cycle is to the next (1 = perfectly periodic, the "synth whine"; a real engine wanders, most at idle and on overrun).
