DJ chatter

Sample radio talk from three stations, to hear whether it lands before any of it goes in the game. The script is tools/dj-voice/lines.json; the voices were made with Qwen3-TTS (tools/dj-voice/README.md). Clean plays the recording as it is; Car stereo plays it the way a car radio would. Mark lines 👍 or 👎 as you go, then Copy my notes and paste them to Claude.

Tap any ▶ button to listen.
