Radio

The game's stations (docs/vision/radio.md 7): streams of generated music on the wall clock, in twenty-minute blocks, the same on every device. Tune away and back and the song has moved on. This is the player the game runs (mp_music.wasm), so what you hear here is what the car hears.

Tap a station to start.
Keeps playing with the screen off; play, pause and next on the lock screen. Add it to the Home Screen to have it as an app.

The dial

level

Energy is what the game drives from the race (a pursuit, speed, a standstill): below full, layers drop out and the mix closes down. Here it grows over each song instead, from half at the start to full by two thirds in; drag the slider to take it over.
