Radio

The game's stations (docs/vision/radio.md 7): streams of generated music on the wall clock, in twenty-minute blocks, the same on every device. Tune away and back and the song has moved on. This is the player the game runs (mp_music.wasm), so what you hear here is what the car hears.

Tap a station to start.
