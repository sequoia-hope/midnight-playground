Police radio voice

The Hot Pursuit dispatcher, recorded with Qwen3-TTS. Every button plays something: ▶ Clean is the recording as it is, ▶ Radio is how it sounds in the game (through the police radio: crunchy, thin, with squelch clicks). Tap a playing button again to stop it.

Tap any ▶ button to listen.

1. The voices to choose from

Each one was designed from the description on its card. The green one is the voice the game uses. To switch, set "voice" in tools/radio-voice/voices.json and run tools/radio-voice/render.py (or just ask Claude).
