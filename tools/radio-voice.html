Midnight Racer — police radio voice

Hot Pursuit's dispatch lines, recorded with Qwen3-TTS by tools/radio-voice/. Raw plays a clip as recorded; Radio plays it the way the game does, through the radio bus (crunch, 300–3000 Hz, squelch and hiss). To try another voice: set "voice" in tools/radio-voice/voices.json and run render.py (see the README).
