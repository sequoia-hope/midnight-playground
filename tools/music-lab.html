Music Lab

A prototype of the music in docs/vision/sound.md: the game's songs re-voiced on emulated classic instruments (TB-303, Juno-style pads, Moog-style monos, DX-style FM, TR-808 and TR-909 drums) with a proper mix, and new house and techno tracks made from genre grammars. B is the new sound; A is the game's music today, playing in step, for comparison.

Make a track

A seed and a genre make a whole track. Déjà vu (after Mutable Instruments' Marbles) is how much the patterns repeat from one 8-bar block to the next: low wanders, high locks into a loop.

Spectrum (what you hear, after the speaker setting)

Measure (offline, no ears)

Renders 12 s from the middle of every song, A and B, and compares loudness (RMS), peak, crest factor (peak over RMS: low is squashed, high is spiky), stereo width (side over mid) and the balance of the bands against the low band (40–150 Hz).
